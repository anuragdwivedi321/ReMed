1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

India faces a profound paradox in its healthcare ecosystem. On one end, low-to-middle-income families face severe financial toxicity due to out-of-pocket expenditure on essential medicines (antibiotics, anti-hypertensives, analgesics, diabetic formulations). On the other end, Indian urban households discard an estimated ₹10,000+ Crores worth of unexpired, intact medicines every year into domestic waste bins.

Discarded pharmaceuticals end up in open municipal landfills, causing active chemical compounds to leach into the groundwater table, contaminating agricultural soil and accelerating hazardous antimicrobial resistance (AMR).

The Core Mission: ReMeD establishes a secure, compliant, zero-friction gateway where citizens can turn sealed, unexpired medicines into instant UPI cashback or charitable clinic donations, backed by certified pharmacist inspection and contactless doorstep logistics.

3. Core Modules & Functional Breakdown

3.1 Intelligent Medicine Search & Catalog Matching

  • Integrated database catalog of 7,000+ CDSCO-approved medicines spanning tablets, capsules, syrups, injections, and ointments.
  • Fuzzy-search algorithm instantly suggests brand names, salt compositions, standard retail prices, and manufacturer classifications.
  • Non-intrusive running text suggestions designed for low visual fatigue.

3.2 Dynamic Buyback Valuation Engine

The platform employs a deterministic mathematical valuation model calculating guaranteed buyback pricing based on remaining shelf life:

  • Tier 1 (> 12 Months remaining): Up to 60% - 70% cashback of printed strip MRP.
  • Tier 2 (6 to 12 Months remaining): Up to 50% cashback of printed strip MRP.
  • Tier 3 (3 to 6 Months remaining): Up to 35% cashback of printed strip MRP.
  • Tier 4 (< 3 Months / Under 30 Days): Buyback disabled for safety; routed to free charitable clinic donation or eco-friendly recycling.
  • Expired Medicines: Strictly rejected by automatic date validation algorithms.

3.3 Multimodal AI Medicine Vision Scanner & "Verified" Result Window

  • Dual camera capture interface: Photo 1 captures whole blister packaging; Photo 2 captures macro date/batch imprint.
  • Multimodal AI inspects foil reflections, embossed lot codes, and expiration stamps.
  • Dedicated "AI Verified" Result Window: A clean card rendering immediately after scanning, displaying:
    • Medicine Name & Salt Composition: e.g., Dolo 650mg (Paracetamol 650mg)
    • Manufacturing Date (MFD): e.g., 03/2024
    • Expiry Date (EXP): e.g., 11/2026 with verified eligibility badge
    • Batch Number: e.g., DL-8492
    • Printed Strip MRP: e.g., ₹34.00
    • Guaranteed Payout Quote: e.g., ₹19.00 (Instant UPI)
    • Official Seal: Green ✓ Verified compliance badge
  • 1-click auto-syncing of all detected data into editable form fields with zero manual typing required.

3.4 Doorstep Collection & Live GPS Tracking

  • Reverse logistics routing with address auto-detection, address category tags (Home, Work, Other), and PIN code serviceability validation.
  • Assigned certified doorstep executive profile display (Driver Name, Rating, Eco-Scooter status, and Direct Call / WhatsApp actions).
  • High-Security Handover OTP: Unique dynamic 4-digit code generated per booking, shared strictly upon physical pharmacist inspection.
  • Real-time visual route simulation with remaining ETA calculation.

3.5 Instant UPI Payout Gateway & NPCI Digital Receipt

  • Regex-based automated VPA validation supporting handles like @oksbi, @paytm, @ybl, @okhdfcbank, @apl, @upi.
  • 12-Digit NPCI UTR Generation: Authentic Unique Transaction Reference generation adhering to standard banking clearing guidelines.
  • Instant Digital Payment Slip Modal: Complete transaction slip with issuing bank routing, beneficiary UPI, copyable UTR, timestamp, shareable message, and printable PDF export.

4. Patient Safety, Quality Control & Legal Framework

Regulatory Adherence: Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) & Drugs and Cosmetics Act (1940).
  1. Zero Expired Medicine Resale: Strict digital validation gates prevent expired drugs from entering the chain.
  2. Tamper-Evident Packaging Verification: Only intact, sealed blister strips with undamaged foil are accepted for buyback. Loose pills and opened bottles are prohibited.
  3. Licensed Pharmacist Verification: Returned medicines are inspected by qualified pharmacists before redistribution to licensed retail or charitable channels.
  4. Schedule H & X Governance: Controlled prescription medications require medical prescription verification prior to secondary redistribution.
  5. Charitable Clinic Redistribution: Medicines donated by users are provided free of cost to economically vulnerable patients with digital donation certificates.
